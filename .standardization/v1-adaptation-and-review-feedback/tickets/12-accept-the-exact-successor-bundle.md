## Question

Does the project maintainer accept the exact version-materialized successor candidate bytes, complete release record, documented limitations and deferred compound-semantic item, compatibility classification, validation evidence, and planned publication and propagation actions as the next Web Editor Revisions release, or is revision required before acceptance?

## Resolution

### Decision

The project maintainer accepted Web Editor Revisions version `1.0.1` as the exact successor bundle on 2026-08-30 and requested no revision. The accepted result includes the complete [publication index](../candidate/README.md), [core standard](../candidate/standard.md), three profiles, evaluation package, curated evidence, retained decisions and provenance, deferred compound-semantic limitation, and [release record](../candidate/release.md).

The 28-file materialized bundle had pre-acceptance SHA-256 manifest digest `2cffdc201006780a049a1675f1128f57153d6619f2a82dbf373a42c69a227115`. The required acceptance record produced digest `20c036ad7eeb2bdef5d1692e012fb84f11846792bae0705ff77256d1a6697d33`. The maintainer subsequently and explicitly replaced side-by-side storage with single-current tagged history, directed predecessor-directory removal, and authorized execution without another confirmation. Only operational storage, authorization, propagation-plan wording, the resulting pre-release Pages verification record, and whitespace hygiene changed; the normative release content did not. The final accepted-bundle digest is `85426094e1215638aeca8ea37f09fbcf4a626559cc9673a7b4b9daab3f357219`.

The accepted-release validator passes. Candidate state is therefore `accepted`; later reports cannot join this release and must begin a new revision or reassessment.

### Rationale and evidence

Acceptance follows complete working-agent and independent version reviews, confirmed patch classification, future-location link and anchor validation for all 22 Markdown files, all 13 core checks, profile catalog validation, the 12-positive/11-negative profile self-test, byte-identical protected artifacts, unchanged capability matrices, complete change traceability, explicit no-migration effect, curated removal of stale v1 release material, and a bounded current-site cutover plan.

The bundle visibly preserves the principal uncertainty: compound multi-paragraph and multi-range proposals, groups or hierarchy, and mutable pending-change lineage remain a high-priority deferred normative area rather than being implied by this patch.

### Alternatives and trade-offs

- **Require revision before acceptance:** was not selected because validation found no missing fact, normative contradiction, broken link, stale current metadata, harmful duplication, or unresolved release field other than the acceptance decision itself.
- **Accept without the deferred compound item:** would make the bundle smaller but would hide a material limitation and lose the reassessment trigger accepted during Assessment.
- **Fold Git mutation or deployment into acceptance:** was not selected. Bundle acceptance establishes immutable content; release commit, tag, and deployment remain separately revalidated cutover operations.

### Uncertainty and follow-up

Acceptance does not prove live native-adapter adoption, universal round-trip success, permanent stewardship, or successful external propagation. [Release, propagate, and close the accepted successor](13-release-propagate-and-close-the-successor.md) must revalidate this digest, isolate the release change from unrelated repository state, promote the candidate unchanged, update and verify the current site, create and verify the authorized commit and immutable tag, observe deployment, complete operational release metadata, and close only after required propagation succeeds.
